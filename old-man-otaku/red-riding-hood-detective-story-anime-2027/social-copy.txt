NEW ANIME: Red Riding Hood is a detective?!

Red Riding Hood: A Detective Story is officially getting a TV anime in 2027. The fantasy-mystery series reimagines Little Red Riding Hood as an investigator solving crimes tied to familiar fairy-tale worlds.

Confirmed: TV anime, 2027 premiere year, announcement dated August 10, 2026. An exact premiere season, cast and production details were not included in the announcement RogueVerse is using for this report.

The hook is simple and strong: fairy tales become crime scenes, familiar characters become suspects, and the girl who once needed to fear the wolf becomes the person asking the questions.
