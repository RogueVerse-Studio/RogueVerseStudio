Haikyu!! Sets Up a 2027 Double Attack With VS The Little Giant and Where the Monsters Go

Hinata vs. Hoshiumi gets a new concept visual while Bokuto vs. Kiryu finally moves in fresh footage. Haikyu!! is preparing two major Nationals quarterfinal stories for 2027.

Haikyu!! is not returning with just one major 2027 project. The franchise is preparing a theatrical continuation and a separate television special that together tackle two of the biggest remaining quarterfinal matches from Nationals.

The latest promotional push gives both projects fresh material. Haikyu!! The Movie: VS The Little Giant has a new concept visual built around Shoyo Hinata and Korai Hoshiumi, while Haikyu!! Where the Monsters Go has received new action footage centered on Kotaro Bokuto and Wakatsu Kiryu.

VS The Little Giant Puts Hinata Across From Hoshiumi

The new concept visual frames Hinata and Hoshiumi across the court, reinforcing the central idea behind Karasuno's quarterfinal against Kamomedai. Both players challenge the assumption that height determines who can dominate at the net, but they arrive at that answer through very different paths.

The movie remains scheduled for Japanese theaters in 2027. Susumu Mitsunaka returns as director and writer, with Production I.G continuing animation production.

Where the Monsters Go Is No Side Dish

The companion television special focuses on another Nationals quarterfinal: Fukurodani vs. Mujinazaka. Its headline matchup is Bokuto against Kiryu, two elite aces whose approaches to pressure and responsibility make the match much more than a simple power contest.

The latest PV is important because it uses finished action footage rather than functioning only as an announcement teaser. The new material shows Bokuto and Kiryu trading attacks and gives viewers a clearer look at how the special is translating the manga's explosive court movement into animation.

Two Quarterfinals, Two Different Kinds of Pressure

Splitting these matches across a movie and television special gives the franchise room to preserve more of Nationals than a single theatrical storyline could comfortably carry. Karasuno vs. Kamomedai is inseparable from Hinata's evolving idea of what it means to become the Little Giant. Fukurodani vs. Mujinazaka, meanwhile, gives Bokuto a stage where his growth as an ace can stand on its own.

That makes the 2027 lineup feel less like one sequel with bonus material and more like a coordinated double feature for two different corners of the tournament.

The Pressure on the Movie Format Hasn't Disappeared

Haikyu!! The Dumpster Battle proved that the franchise can turn a major manga match into a successful theatrical event. It also intensified the debate over how much of Haruichi Furudate's remaining story can fit into feature-length adaptations without losing smaller character beats.

The existence of Where the Monsters Go is therefore encouraging. A separate special creates another lane for material that does not belong inside Karasuno's movie while still deserving full animation treatment.

The Old Man Otaku Take

The smartest thing about this update is that Haikyu!! is letting both matches breathe. Hinata vs. Hoshiumi is the obvious theatrical hook, but Bokuto vs. Kiryu is exactly the kind of matchup that could have been squeezed into a montage if the production were racing only toward the ending.

Instead, 2027 is shaping up as a two-court attack. One project asks what happens when Hinata finally meets another player carrying the Little Giant ideal. The other lets two of Japan's most dangerous high-school aces collide without Karasuno needing to be in the room.

RogueVerse takeaway: the movie is the headline, but the TV special may be the clearest sign that the anime still wants to give Nationals more room than one film can provide.
