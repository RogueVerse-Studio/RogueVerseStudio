Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2 Trailer Unleashes the Sky Island Arc Ahead of October Premiere

Fran and Teacher are heading skyward. The newest Season 2 trailer reveals the Sky Island Arc, a dangerous new Lich, both theme songs and a release calendar that needs one important correction.

Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2 just gave fans their clearest look yet at Fran and Teacher's next adventure. A newly released trailer and key visual preview the upcoming Sky Island Arc, introduce another major threat and finally put the season's opening and ending music into the spotlight.

The new material arrives with an important schedule clarification. Japanese television broadcasts do not begin September 30. That date belongs to ABEMA's one-week-early exclusive streaming release in Japan. The Japanese TV broadcast begins October 7, with additional stations joining on October 9.

The New Trailer Is Pointing Toward the Sky Island Arc

The latest promotional video shifts the focus beyond simply reminding viewers that Season 2 exists. Anime Corner identifies the material as a preview of the Sky Island Arc, with the new key visual placing Fran and Teacher inside a much larger airborne adventure.

That matters because the first season built its appeal around the relationship between Fran and Teacher as much as the fights themselves. Fran is still the visible adventurer swinging the blade, but Teacher is mentor, partner, weapon and surrogate family all at once. Season 2 now has the opportunity to push that bond into a more dangerous environment with larger threats and a wider world around them.

A New Lich Joins the Fight

The official Japanese announcement confirms veteran voice actor Show Hayami as the Lich. The new PV and visual lean heavily into undead imagery, giving the next stage of the journey a darker fantasy flavor than a simple sightseeing trip into the clouds.

Fran and Teacher have already dealt with monsters, dungeons and enemies who underestimate a Black Cat girl carrying an unusually opinionated sword. An undead-heavy conflict gives Season 2 a different visual texture immediately: skeletal enemies, supernatural threats and a Lich positioned as a major new obstacle.

FZMZ Opens the Season With “DREAM OF BUTTERFLY”

The trailer previews the new opening theme, “DREAM OF BUTTERFLY” by FZMZ. The official anime site ties the song to the philosophical idea of the butterfly dream, an unusually fitting theme for a story whose central character literally woke up reincarnated as a sword.

The ending theme is “Baribari Buddy” by HANABIE. Together, the songs give Season 2 two distinct musical identities while keeping the focus on transformation, identity and Fran and Teacher's partnership.

The Production Team Is Staying Familiar

Studio C2C returns to animate the sequel, with Shinji Ishihira directing. Takahiro Nagano returns for series composition and scripts, Atsuko Saito returns on character design, and Yasuharu Takanashi is back on music.

That continuity matters because Season 1 found a recognizable balance between fantasy action, comedy and the almost parent-and-child warmth of Fran and Teacher's relationship. A returning core staff gives Season 2 a better chance of expanding the world without losing that center.

The Corrected Release Calendar

The official Japanese schedule now separates streaming and television clearly. ABEMA begins exclusive one-week-early streaming on September 30 at 24:30 JST. Japanese television broadcasting begins October 7 on TOKYO MX and ABC TV. BS Asahi and AT-X begin their broadcasts on October 9.

  • September 30, 2026 — ABEMA early exclusive streaming in Japan begins
  • October 7, 2026 — TOKYO MX and ABC TV broadcasts begin
  • October 9, 2026 — BS Asahi and AT-X broadcasts begin

September 30 was never a meaningless date. It was simply being attached to the wrong release method in some coverage.

HIDIVE Still Has the International Streaming Story

HIDIVE previously confirmed that it will stream Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2 exclusively as part of its Fall 2026 lineup. That gives the sequel a clear North American streaming home while Japan follows its separate ABEMA-first and television schedule.

International fans should not treat September 30 as a universal launch time. HIDIVE has confirmed the Fall 2026 simulcast, but regional timing should be followed through HIDIVE's own schedule as the premiere approaches.

Why This Trailer Matters More Than Another Release-Date Reminder

Season 2 was announced after the first season ended, giving fans a long wait for genuinely new material. This trailer finally offers more than another “coming soon” message. It gives the sequel a recognizable arc, a new villain, a stronger visual identity and music that can start defining the season before episode one airs.

For a returning isekai series competing inside a crowded Fall 2026 season, specificity matters. “Fran and Teacher are back” is good news. “Fran and Teacher are heading into the Sky Island Arc against undead forces and a Lich” is a reason to remember the date.

The Old Man Otaku Take

Reincarnated as a Sword has always had a wonderfully ridiculous elevator pitch: a man is reincarnated as a weapon, gets picked up by a young catgirl adventurer and somehow becomes one of fantasy anime's more wholesome mentor figures. The joke gets you through the door. Fran and Teacher's relationship is what makes people stay.

The newest Season 2 material looks smart because it does not try to reinvent that appeal. It widens the playground. The Sky Island setting, undead enemies and new Lich create a bigger stage while the returning staff and cast keep the emotional center familiar.

RogueVerse takeaway: September 30 is the ABEMA early-streaming date. October 7 is the Japanese TV premiere. And the bigger news is that Season 2 finally looks like a specific adventure rather than a sequel still hiding behind a release window.
