10 Most Liked New Manga of 2026

The breakout hits, hidden gems and community favorites that RogueVerse's research set says are dominating manga conversations this year.

ROGUEVERSE MANGA FEATURE

10 MOST LIKED NEW MANGA OF 2026

THE TITLES TAKING OVER THE CONVERSATION

By RogueVerse Studio | August 6, 2026

2026 has delivered a deep field of manga competing for attention across Japan and international fandom. For this feature, RogueVerse is not treating “most liked” as a synonym for raw sales. The ranking in our research package combines critical reception, reader scores, community discussion, recommendation frequency, growth since launch, international popularity and long-term potential.

Methodology note: This article follows the RogueVerse research package compiled from critic reviews, reader ratings, sales momentum, Manga Plus engagement, BookWalker trends, Japanese bookstore rankings, Reddit discussion, MAL community reception, Anime Corner, Anime News Network, Comic Natalie, Oricon, Kono Manga ga Sugoi discussion and additional manga/news community sources.

10. Nomads

Independent Original Manga

Although still independent, Nomads has quietly begun attracting attention among indie manga communities because of its Western sci-fi adventure mixed with anime storytelling.

Readers have praised its expressive characters, cinematic action, strong world building and emotional friendships. If development continues, the project has the ingredients to become a breakout independent manga over the next few years.

Why people like it:

  • Adventure
  • Humor
  • Character chemistry
  • Strong art direction

9. Phantom Protocol

Cyberpunk action continues to have a strong pulse in 2026, and Phantom Protocol stands out in the RogueVerse research package for mixing espionage and AI warfare with emotional character writing.

Its strongest point is pacing. Nearly every chapter is built around forward motion and a strong closing beat, giving the series the kind of momentum that fuels weekly discussion.

8. Wild Hearts

Wild Hearts arrives from a different corner of the field: romance grounded in young adults balancing careers, relationships and dreams.

The appeal is relatability. Instead of leaning entirely on heightened romantic gimmicks, the series is praised in the source package for dialogue and emotional situations that feel recognizable enough to spread naturally on social platforms.

7. Leviathan Code

Space archaeology, ancient civilizations and survival horror collide in Leviathan Code.

Its hook is discovery. Readers are pulled forward by secrets hidden inside abandoned alien structures and the slow reveal of what those ruins mean. In a year full of action-forward manga, its mystery-first identity helps it occupy a different lane.

6. Iron Blood Academy

Battle-school manga are hardly rare, but Iron Blood Academy earns its place by focusing on friendships and growth before the major tournament machinery takes over.

That investment in the cast is the point. Readers become attached to the students before the biggest battles begin, making later conflict feel personal instead of mechanical.

5. Clockwork Eden

Clockwork Eden mixes fantasy and steampunk with detailed mechanical cities and a visual identity that immediately separates it from the crowd.

Its strongest talking points are its backgrounds, memorable villains and creative magic system. The art is not merely decoration here. It is one of the core reasons readers remember the series.

4. Black Torch Revival Edition

The return of Black Torch became a major rediscovery story in the RogueVerse research package. New readers found the series while longtime fans had a reason to bring it back into the conversation.

The comeback has helped younger readers experience a title whose art, monster designs and character chemistry always felt larger than its original run.

3. Kagurabachi

Kagurabachi is not a 2026 debut, but 2026 represents the point where its momentum became impossible to ignore in this ranking package.

Anime news, sales growth and viral discussion pushed the sword-action series deeper into the mainstream. Its cinematic fight choreography remains one of its biggest calling cards.

2. Centuria

Centuria brings dark fantasy, haunting artwork, morally gray characters and a world that rarely feels safe.

Its unpredictability is central to its appeal. The series gives readers the sense that consequences are real and that the story is willing to take uncomfortable turns rather than protect its cast.

1. Ichi the Witch

Ichi the Witch takes the top spot in the RogueVerse ranking package because it balances fantasy, comedy, emotional storytelling and spectacular artwork without allowing one element to swallow the others.

Its characters became a major part of the appeal before action alone had to carry the series. That balance has helped turn it into one of the fastest-growing manga conversations of the year.

What the list says about manga in 2026

A few patterns repeat across the ranking package. Fantasy remains dominant, but readers are responding strongly to character-driven stories rather than power progression alone. Dark fantasy continues to generate intense discussion, while titles with unmistakable visual identities travel especially well through TikTok, Shorts and Instagram.

There is also more room for independent work to enter the conversation. Digital distribution and creator-focused platforms have lowered some of the walls that once kept original projects away from the same social spaces occupied by established publishers.

The bigger lesson is that “most liked” is becoming harder to reduce to one scoreboard. Sales matter. Ratings matter. Community discussion matters. So does how quickly a title becomes part of the culture surrounding manga rather than simply another release on the shelf.

RogueVerse Take

If 2026 has a defining manga trend, it may be that readers are rewarding identity. The titles generating the most affection are not simply the ones with the loudest fights. They are the ones readers can recognize instantly, describe to a friend in one sentence and keep talking about after the chapter ends.

That is why Ichi the Witch, Centuria and Kagurabachi sit so high in this RogueVerse ranking, while a project such as Nomads can still earn a place in the same conversation through a very different route.
