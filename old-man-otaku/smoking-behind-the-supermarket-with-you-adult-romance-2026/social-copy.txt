TIKTOK / REELS / SHORTS CAPTION
Some of us have watched enough anime romances about teenagers taking 12 episodes to hold hands.

We have jobs now.

Smoking Behind the Supermarket with You feels different because its romance is built around something anime does not explore nearly enough: adult exhaustion.

Sasaki is a 45-year-old office worker whose daily life has basically ground him into powder. One of his few comforts is talking to Yamada, the friendly cashier at his local supermarket. Then he starts spending his evenings smoking behind the building with the much more blunt Tayama.

Except Yamada and Tayama are the same woman, and Sasaki somehow does not realize it.

What makes the series work is not only that joke. It is the way their relationship grows through tiny moments: a bad day, a conversation and five extra minutes before going home.
