Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans Locks in October Premiere With New Trailer

Takemichi is going back for Mikey, and the anime has officially entered launch mode for its October return.

Tokyo Revengers is preparing to throw Takemichi back into one of the most dangerous periods of his story. The official anime team released the fourth promotional video for War of the Three Titans Arc on August 24, giving viewers another look at Takemichi’s attempt to save Mikey while the production moves into its final stretch before broadcast.

More importantly, the date is locked: October 2, 2026. Japanese broadcasts begin through MBS, TBS and CBC’s Animeism block, with BS-TBS and AT-X following. Disney+ is also listed as the worldwide subscription-streaming exclusive outside mainland China.

The new trailer features “IGNITE” by JO1, the arc’s opening theme. The ending theme is go!go!vanillas’ “Trickster.”

Takemichi Is Going Back for Mikey

The new footage reinforces the central problem facing Takemichi. After everything he has already changed through time travel, saving everyone still has not produced the future he wanted. Mikey remains the exception.

That sends Takemichi back into the past again, but the landscape waiting for him is no longer the Tokyo Manji Gang environment that defined the beginning of the series. The new arc revolves around Tokyo’s three major powers and brings Senju Kawaragi, Brahman and South Terano’s Rokuhara Tandai deeper into the anime’s conflict.

Mikey Remains the Center

Previous promotional material has increasingly focused on Mikey, including a trailer showing his future self alongside Haruchiyo Sanzu. The newest promotion brings the focus back to Takemichi’s determination to save him.

That creates an important emotional inversion. Earlier conflicts often gave Takemichi a specific catastrophe to prevent. Mikey is a different challenge. The question is not simply whether Takemichi can stop one fight or one death. It is whether he can reach someone who keeps moving farther away from the person he remembers.

The Three Titans Change the Board

The anime has already introduced additional players for this stage of the story. Brahman figures Takeomi Akashi, Wakasa Imaushi and Keizo Arashi join Senju, while South Terano leads Rokuhara Tandai. Their arrival turns the new storyline into a broader struggle for power after the upheaval of the previous arcs.

That is what makes this continuation more than another reset. Takemichi is entering a fractured power structure shaped by everything that came before, while the consequences of earlier timelines continue converging around Mikey.

RogueVerse Take

The fourth trailer matters because it arrives as part of a larger launch push. The premiere date, opening and ending themes, broadcast schedule and international streaming plan are now all in place.

War of the Three Titans has moved from “upcoming continuation” into launch mode. Takemichi has changed the past again and again to save the people around him. This time, the person at the center of the rescue is Mikey himself.

Tokyo Revengers: War of the Three Titans Arc premieres October 2, 2026.
