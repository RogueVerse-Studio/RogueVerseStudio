## Caption
AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY has enough heroes to fill several movies, so why do Steve Rogers and Reed Richards feel like two of the characters Marvel may use to guide us through the chaos?

The comic-book logic is stronger than it first looks.

Reed Richards is the MAP. Incursions, impossible science, the Illuminati, Battleworld and especially Doctor Doom are baked into some of Reed's most important Marvel stories. If universes are colliding, Reed is the character who can explain what is happening and what options remain.

Steve Rogers is the COMPASS. He does not need to be the smartest or strongest person in the room. His real superpower has always been getting wildly different heroes to trust one another long enough to become a team.

Then there is Doom.

Doom is the THIRD ANSWER: CONTROL.

Reed says we can solve this.
Steve says we can survive without abandoning who we are.
Doom says both of you failed, so give me the power to decide what survives.

That is why this pairing could work so well. Reed understands the apocalypse. Steve understands the people trapped inside it. Doom believes only he deserves to decide what comes after.

And Steve returning does NOT have to erase Sam Wilson. Sam is Captain America. Steve can function as the veteran strategist while Sam remains the man carrying the mantle forward.
