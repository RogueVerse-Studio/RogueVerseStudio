The beginning of the RogueVerse

OUR STORY

A creator-owned studio does not start with a giant building. It starts with a reason to make one.

RogueVerse Studio began with Omo's love of anime, manga, pop culture, writing, and drawing, plus a stubborn belief that original ideas should not have to wait for permission to exist.

The studio is being built one story at a time. RVS News covers the culture that inspires us. RVS Publishing develops the work we create. RogueVerse Studio: Mythra gives those stories a shelf and readers a place to experience them directly.

This is the early chapter. The website, catalog, and final mission statement will grow in public as the RogueVerse takes shape.

From the creator

A MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

I’m Omo, founder and creative director of RogueVerse Studio. I’m a writer, artist, storyteller, and lifelong fan of anime, manga, comics, movies, and gaming.

I created RogueVerse Studio to build the kind of creative space I always wanted to see—one where original stories, independent creators, entertainment news, and bold ideas can exist under the same roof. Through RogueVerse Mythra, I’m developing original worlds and series while learning every part of the process, from the first idea to the finished page.

RogueVerse Studio is still growing, but that’s part of the story. I’m not waiting for someone else to open the door. I’m building my own.

— Omo
Founder & Creative Director, RogueVerse Studio
