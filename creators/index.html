CREATOR GUIDELINES

Your story. Your world. Our legacy. RogueVerse Mythra is building a home for original manga, comics, webtoons and visual storytelling. These guidelines explain what to prepare before reaching out and the standards we expect from every potential collaboration.

Your Story

You bring the vision. We help build the path forward.

Partnership

Clear communication, transparency and mutual respect come first.

Creator First

Credit, rights and responsibilities should be defined clearly before work is accepted.

Original Always

Submissions must be original and free of unauthorized third-party material.

Who We’re Looking For

  • Manga, comic and webtoon creators
  • Writers seeking artists
  • Artists seeking writers
  • Letterers, colorists and designers
  • Small teams and creative groups
  • Original projects with long-term potential

All skill levels may inquire. Passion, reliability and potential matter.

Submission Readiness Checklist

  • Project title
  • Short synopsis
  • Genre and intended audience
  • Creator or team biography
  • Sample artwork or 5–10 finished pages when available
  • Planned format and estimated chapter or issue length
  • Current production status
  • Portfolio or social links
  • Confirmation that you control the submitted work

What We Do Not Accept

  • Fan fiction or unauthorized derivative projects
  • Plagiarized or stolen work
  • Projects built around copyrighted IP you do not control
  • Uncredited material from other creators
  • Projects with unclear ownership
  • Deceptive presentation of AI-assisted material

How Partnership Can Work

Mythra Originals: projects developed directly with RogueVerse Studio.

Independent Publishing: original creator projects hosted or promoted through Mythra under an agreed publishing arrangement.

Limited Features: one-shots, pilots, previews and anthology appearances.

Creative Collaboration: writers, artists, editors and designers connecting around original projects.

Rights & Agreements

Ownership, publishing rights, promotional usage, exclusivity, revenue terms, credit and termination conditions will be defined in writing before any project is formally accepted.

Nothing is accepted on the basis of vague or implied rights. The agreement comes first.

Our Creator Code

  • Be original, honest and respectful
  • Communicate professionally
  • Credit collaborators clearly
  • Meet agreed deadlines and commitments
  • Use AI tools responsibly and disclose meaningful AI-assisted material
  • Help build a positive creative community

Quick FAQ

Does the work need to be finished?

No. Projects can be at different stages, but the inquiry should clearly explain what exists now and what still needs to be produced.

Can a writer apply without an artist?

Yes. Writers and artists may inquire independently or as teams.

Does Mythra accept webtoons?

Yes. Mythra is open to manga, comics, webtoons and other original illustrated-story formats.

Are fan comics accepted?

No. Submissions should be original works that do not depend on unauthorized copyrighted characters, settings or franchises.

Who owns the story?

Rights will be stated in the written agreement for each project. RogueVerse will not treat unclear ownership as permission.
